How it works

From order to done, in four steps.

Kirana stores, supermarkets and neighbourhood shops that already take WhatsApp or phone orders and want them in one tidy system.

  1. Customer orders on their phone

    They browse your categories and prices, apply a coupon and check out, then track the order live.

  2. Shop accepts and packs

    The shop desk shows new orders, products, stock and today’s sales in one place.

  3. Delivery partner picks up

    Partners go online, accept requests and see their earnings and cash in hand.

  4. Handover with OTP

    The customer shares an OTP at the door, so every delivery is confirmed.
